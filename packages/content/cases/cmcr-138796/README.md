## Learning outcomes

After the case the learner can:

1. Reconstruct a multi-organ timeline in relative time showing that
   proteinuria, haematuria and a raised creatinine preceded both abdominal
   operations.
2. Diagnose pauci-immune RPGN from a strongly positive MPO-ANCA, negative
   anti-GBM, ANA and anti-dsDNA and an immunofluorescence-negative crescentic
   biopsy, and state the limits of a five-glomerulus sample.
3. Recognise gastrointestinal involvement by ANCA-associated vasculitis
   presenting as appendicitis-like pain and adult intussusception, and ask
   pathology to re-review resected specimens.
4. Plan induction against KDIGO 2024 (glucocorticoids with cyclophosphamide
   or rituximab; plasma exchange considered above 300 μmol/L with dialysis
   need; no delay for the biopsy) and compare it with the report's regimen.
5. Put the supportive and safety measures in place: dialysis, co-trimoxazole
   prophylaxis (EULAR 2022), infection vigilance after a recent colectomy,
   and pregnancy and fertility before cyclophosphamide.

These are the case's `learningOutcomes`; the six HUD `objectives` below are
the in-game task list.

## Clinical summary

**Before the transfer (relative to day 0).**

| when | what |
|---|---|
| ≈ 8 weeks (57 days) before | local hospital, for leg swelling with dark urine, fatigue, anorexia and epigastric fullness: BP 180/100 mmHg, urine protein ++, red cells 80–100/HPF, **creatinine 261 μmol/L**; antihypertensive and diuretic, oedema eased |
| 6 weeks (42 days) before | abdominal pain without diarrhoea, fever, night sweats, mucus or blood → "acute appendicitis" → appendicectomy (local hospital) |
| ≈ 1 month before | renal clinic (this hospital): BP 160/100, protein +++, red cells too many to count, **24-h protein 3.61 g, creatinine 351 μmol/L**; amlodipine 5 mg/day |
| > 20 days before | urine output falling |
| 18 days before | pain with nausea, vomiting and diarrhoea (no melaena, arthralgia, fever or purpura) → ED → CT: **ascending-colon intussusception** → laparoscopic ascending colectomy |
| surgical stay | urinalysis unchanged; urine output 80–100 mL/24 h; creatinine 679 μmol/L; **pANCA positive, anti-MPO 115,000 RU/L (< 20,000)**, anti-GBM, ANA and anti-dsDNA negative; no propylthiouracil-type drugs |

**Day 0 (renal ward).** BP 130/70 mmHg, HR 88/min; pale skin, mucosa and
conjunctivae; heart and lungs normal; abdomen soft with binder, dressing clean
and dry; no leg oedema; tone and power normal, no pathological reflexes.
WBC 12 × 10⁹/L, Hb 85 g/L, platelets 332 × 10⁹/L; ESR 78 mm/h; CRP 15.3 mg/L
(< 8); **creatinine 700 μmol/L, urea 7.88 mmol/L (as printed)**; urine protein
+++, 24-h protein 2.31 g, red cells 30–40/HPF. Ultrasound: kidneys 9.7 and
9.9 cm, slightly increased parenchymal echogenicity.

**Day 9.** Biopsy: immunofluorescence negative; 5 glomeruli with severe tuft
disruption — 2 cellular, 2 fibrocellular, 1 fibrous crescent; tubular
atrophy; interstitial oedema, focal infiltrate and fibrosis; thickened
arterioles; type III crescentic GN (also on EM). Bowel re-review: mucosal
erosions and ulcers, marked oedema, congested vessels, heavy mixed infiltrate;
periarteriolar eosinophils, lymphocytes and monocytes; occasional fibrinoid
necrosis.

**Diagnosis.** MPO-ANCA–associated small-vessel vasculitis with type III
(pauci-immune) crescentic RPGN, AKI needing dialysis, renal hypertension and
gastrointestinal vasculitis.

**Reported treatment and outcome.** Methylprednisolone 40 mg/day IV,
haemodialysis and supportive care, then methylprednisolone pulses 250, 500 and
500 mg → urine 1,300–1,500 mL/day, creatinine 200 μmol/L, ESR 10 mm/h, CRP 3.43
mg/L, off dialysis; prednisone 50 mg/day; IV cyclophosphamide 0.8 g monthly
from day 13; co-trimoxazole 2 tablets twice weekly. Later: stable, creatinine
120 μmol/L, normal urine tests, ESR and CRP, no abdominal pain. No plasma
exchange or rituximab is recorded.

### Decisions

- `diagnosis` (single; before the biopsy): MPO-ANCA vasculitis with RPGN and
  gut involvement. Distractors: post-operative ATN (the kidney disease
  predates the first operation), IgA vasculitis (no purpura; MPO strongly
  positive), anti-GBM disease, lupus nephritis.
- `induction` (single): start now — methylprednisolone pulses then a reduced
  taper, with cyclophosphamide or rituximab, consider plasma exchange, biopsy
  without delay to treatment. Distractors: glucocorticoids alone; wait for the
  anastomosis to heal; azathioprine induction; plasma exchange instead of
  immunosuppression.
- `supportive` (select all): haemodialysis now with regular reassessment;
  co-trimoxazole prophylaxis; pregnancy and fertility before cyclophosphamide
  plus infection and wound vigilance. Distractors: no prophylaxis because the
  lungs are clear; dialysis only above a fixed creatinine.
- `biopsy-interpretation` (single; day 9): pauci-immune crescentic GN, mostly
  active, five glomeruli → cautious. Distractors: "irreversible — stop",
  "IF negative means not immune-mediated", "repeat the biopsy first".

### How wrong choices score

- Decisions use `decidedFirstTry`.
- `azathioprine` is `contraindicated` unless cyclophosphamide or rituximab has
  been given; `defer-immunosuppression` always is. Both fail
  `r-safety-undertreat` (a harm-avoidance item: the annotation file gives it
  `scoredWhen`, so it will count only once induction has been attempted or a
  drug given).
- `mp-40` (the report's pre-pulse dose) and `plasma-exchange` are indicated
  once the diagnosis is made; rituximab and cyclophosphamide are both
  accepted for the induction objective and rubric.

## 中文摘要

**病例概要。** 36岁女性。入院前约8周因双下肢水肿在当地就诊：血压180/100 mmHg，尿蛋白++、红细胞80–100/HPF，血肌酐261 μmol/L；约2周后因腹痛按急性阑尾炎手术；约1个月前肾内科门诊：24小时尿蛋白3.61 g，肌酐351 μmol/L；18天前再次腹痛，CT示升结肠套叠，行腹腔镜升结肠切除；尿量减至80–100 mL/24 h，肌酐679 μmol/L；pANCA阳性，抗MPO抗体115 000 RU/L，抗GBM、ANA、抗dsDNA阴性。转入肾内科时肌酐700 μmol/L（尿素氮7.88 mmol/L，二者不相称，按原文保留），血红蛋白85 g/L，ESR 78 mm/h，CRP 15.3 mg/L。第9天肾活检：免疫荧光阴性，5个肾小球中2个细胞性、2个细胞纤维性、1个纤维性新月体，Ⅲ型新月体肾炎；复阅肠标本见小动脉周围炎细胞浸润和个别血管纤维素样坏死。诊断：MPO-ANCA相关小血管炎，急进性肾炎，消化道受累。原报告予甲泼尼龙、透析、冲击、泼尼松、环磷酰胺和复方磺胺甲噁唑后脱离透析，肌酐降至120 μmol/L。

**学习目标。**

1. 按相对时间重建病程，证明肾脏异常早于两次腹部手术。
2. 根据MPO-ANCA强阳性、其他抗体阴性和免疫荧光阴性的新月体肾炎诊断寡免疫性急进性肾炎，并说明5个肾小球的局限。
3. 识别ANCA相关血管炎的消化道受累可表现为阑尾炎样腹痛和成人肠套叠，要求复阅既往标本。
4. 依据KDIGO 2024制定诱导方案（激素联合环磷酰胺或利妥昔单抗；肌酐> 300 μmol/L且需透析时考虑血浆置换；不因等待活检推迟），并与原报告方案比较。
5. 安排透析、肺孢子菌预防（EULAR 2022）、术后感染监测，以及环磷酰胺前排除妊娠、讨论生育。

**关键决策。**

- 诊断（活检前）：一元论——MPO-ANCA相关小血管炎；不是术后肾小管坏死，也不像IgA血管炎、抗GBM病或狼疮。
- 诱导：现在就开始——甲泼尼龙冲击后减量，联合环磷酰胺或利妥昔单抗，考虑血浆置换，尽快活检但不等待；不单用激素，不等伤口愈合，不用硫唑嘌呤诱导，血浆置换不能代替免疫抑制。
- 支持与安全（多选）：现在透析并定期评估恢复；复方磺胺甲噁唑预防；环磷酰胺前排除妊娠、讨论生育，并密切观察感染和吻合口。
- 活检解读：寡免疫性新月体肾炎，以活动性病变为主，有恢复可能；5个肾小球结论要谨慎，继续积极诱导。

**复盘要点。**

- KDIGO 2024：病情迅速恶化时不要等活检；激素联合环磷酰胺或利妥昔单抗；肌酐> 300 μmol/L并需透析时考虑血浆置换。
