## Decisions and assessment

- **diagnosis:** How should the early differential be organised? Options: Assess bilirubin-related neurological injury, infection and poisoning while obtaining caregiving and medication history (supported: Marked jaundice does not alone settle all neurological findings, and exposure may be omitted.) Raised bilirubin removes the need to investigate exposure (distractor: Coexisting causes are possible; one abnormality must not end the aetiological assessment.) Normal cranial ultrasound excludes serious cerebral dysfunction (distractor: Structural ultrasound does not exclude metabolic or drug-induced dysfunction.)
- **evidence:** Which conclusion is supported by the subsequent records? Options: Exposure and levels support poisoning without proving a single cause for all neurological signs (supported: Marked bilirubin elevation and the infection record coexist and require integrated assessment.) One drug concentration determines all resuscitation (distractor: Clinical consciousness, breathing and circulation take priority; reported levels supplement evidence.) A concentration rebound proves the infant received another dose because of exchange (distractor: The report discusses redistribution and ongoing absorption; it does not establish repeat ingestion.)
- **management:** Once drug exposure is established, which safety plan is most appropriate? Options: Joint neonatal critical-care and toxicology support for airway/circulation and observation, with safeguarding and safe-care assessment (supported: Severe drug exposure and altered consciousness require specialist support, and prevention of repeat exposure is essential.) Ask relatives to repeat induced vomiting or enemas at home (distractor: Unsupervised decontamination with impaired consciousness risks aspiration and other harm; historical measures cannot simply be copied.) Recovery in one case proves exchange transfusion works for every clozapine poisoning (distractor: Concurrent hyperbilirubinaemia and multiple supportive measures prevent attribution to one treatment.)

## 中文摘要

**病例概要：** 后续照护史及药物浓度证实氯氮平暴露，与高胆红素血症并存；恢复不能单独证明某种清除技术的疗效。

**学习目标：** 在显著黄疸之外识别需补充的药物暴露史；用后续浓度与病史解释中毒证据；区分治疗后浓度反弹与临床恢复；提出包含儿童保护的专业支持计划

**关键决策：** 现有早期资料应怎样组织鉴别？ 后续资料能支持哪一项结论？ 药物暴露明确后，什么安全计划最合适？

**复盘要点：** 迟获的家庭用药史可以改变新生儿脑病的解释；治疗后的浓度反弹提示单次测值不能替代动态观察；儿童保护是后续安全计划的一部分。尚待核对：准确摄入量、完整药代时序、再次换血情况及标准化长期神经发育随访。本稿尚未经执业医师审核。
