## Learning outcomes

1. Recognise the urgency of postoperative visual loss.
2. Explain the limits of an intact bony wall.
3. Distinguish infection evidence from organism confirmation.
4. Plan specialist care without delaying visual protection.

## Clinical summary

The source describes postoperative orbital infection and elevated pressure with blindness, followed by visual recovery after decompression and abscess management. The visual trend establishes urgency beyond a single bony-wall image. Absent retrobulbar haematoma does not exclude a pressure emergency. The source does not establish the organism or route.

## Decisions and assessment

- **diagnosis:** Which judgement matters most with declining vision and proptosis? Options: Suspect a vision-threatening orbital pressure emergency and obtain immediate ophthalmology/ENT involvement (supported: An intact wall and absent haematoma do not exclude dangerous pressure elevation.) An intact bony wall excludes orbital complications (distractor: Infection and pressure injury do not require a visible fracture.) Observe all postoperative swelling until blindness occurs (distractor: Declining vision is already an emergency signal.)
- **evidence:** Which conclusion is supported by the subsequent records? Options: The tense orbit and operative abnormalities support a serious orbital process, but route and organism remain unproven (supported: CT does not establish a particular injection or occult fracture as the sole cause.) Pus alone identifies the bacterial species (distractor: No culture result was supplied.) Possible later recovery proves waiting is safe (distractor: Recovery in one patient cannot negate irreversible visual risk from delay.)
- **management:** What is the safe strategy now? Options: When vision is clinically threatened, arrange immediate trained decompression assessment and infection management without delaying for imaging (supported: Care depends on anatomy and severity; do not copy globe compression or historical fixed very-high steroid doses.) Have the learner press on the globe to lower pressure (distractor: This is not a safe simulated manoeuvre and may worsen injury.) Always wait for repeat CT before consultation regardless of vision (distractor: Clear visual threat must not be delayed by imaging.)

## 中文摘要

**病例概要：** 原报告为鼻窦术后眼眶感染及压力升高伴失明，经减压与后续脓肿处理恢复视力。

**学习目标：** 识别术后视力下降的紧急性。；解释正常骨壁的排除能力限制。；区分感染证据与病原确证。；制定不延误视力保护的专科计划。

**关键决策：** 视力下降并突眼时，哪项判断最重要？ 后续资料能支持哪一项结论？ 现在应采用怎样的安全策略？

**复盘要点：** 视功能趋势比单一骨壁影像更能提示紧迫性；无球后血肿不等于无压力急症；病原和传播途径未由源文证实。尚待核对：缺少准确眼压、瞳孔传入检查和微生物培养；传播机制不确定。本稿尚未经执业医师审核。
