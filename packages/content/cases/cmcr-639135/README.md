## Learning outcomes

1. Reconstruct the sequence of fat loss and diabetes.
2. Compare causes using body habitus, metabolic and endocrine evidence.
3. Identify internal laboratory contradictions and preserve uncertainty.
4. Distinguish historical management from current specialist eligibility and safety assessment.

## Clinical summary

The source diagnosed acquired generalised lipodystrophy; adult-onset fat loss and metabolic findings support that interpretation, with limits to causal classification and some laboratory reliability. Fat loss preceding hyperglycaemia by years helps avoid reducing the case to ordinary diabetes. Absent numbness symptoms and abnormal nerve conduction can coexist. Retaining numerical contradictions is more faithful than silently correcting them.

## Decisions and assessment

- **diagnosis:** Which interpretation best integrates adult-onset fat loss, difficult glycaemic control and severe hypertriglyceridaemia? Options: Consider metabolic disease associated with generalised lipodystrophy; adult onset supports an acquired form (supported: Fat-distribution changes precede hyperglycaemia and must be interpreted with the metabolic phenotype.) Adult onset alone makes ordinary type 2 diabetes sufficient to explain everything (distractor: This does not adequately explain widespread subcutaneous fat loss and extreme triglycerides.) Marked thinning alone establishes hyperthyroidism (distractor: Body change does not establish hyperthyroidism; thyroid-specific evidence is required.)
- **evidence:** Which conclusion is supported by the subsequent records? Options: Body fat and metabolic findings support generalised lipodystrophy, but neither low leptin nor negative family history alone establishes cause; LDL needs verification (supported: Insulin within its fasting reference range does not independently exclude resistance; adult onset supports an acquired form without completing genetic exclusion.) Fasting insulin within its reference range proves there is no insulin resistance (distractor: A single secretion value must be interpreted with hyperglycaemia, fat loss and the clinical context.) Treat LDL 5.90 as verified and build precise lipid conclusions on it (distractor: LDL exceeding total cholesterol and extreme triglycerides require laboratory verification.)
- **management:** Which subsequent management principle is most appropriate in a current review? Options: Arrange endocrine and relevant specialist assessment of glycaemia, triglycerides and organ risks, verify lipids, and review eligibility and risks before considering metreleptin (supported: US approval includes leptin-deficiency complications of congenital or acquired generalised lipodystrophy; this does not directly prescribe for this patient, and antibody, lymphoma and hypoglycaemia risks require assessment.) Because the old report calls leptin experimental, there is no reason to discuss specialist options today (distractor: The historical approval status is outdated; current labelling and local access need checking.) Copy the case fixed insulin and rosiglitazone regimen without reassessing organ risks (distractor: A historical regimen does not establish individual safety today or justify overlooking extreme triglyceride risk.)

## 中文摘要

**病例概要：** 原报告诊断获得性全身性脂肪萎缩；成人后体脂丢失和代谢表现支持此方向，但病因分型及部分检验可靠性仍有限。

**学习目标：** 重建体脂变化与糖尿病的先后顺序。；用体型、代谢和内分泌证据比较病因。；发现检验内部矛盾并保留不确定性。；区分历史管理与现行专科适应证及安全评估。

**关键决策：** 哪种解释最能整合成年后体脂减少、难控制血糖和严重高甘油三酯？ 后续资料能支持哪一项结论？ 当前复盘中，哪项后续管理原则最合适？

**复盘要点：** 体脂减少在高血糖前多年出现，有助于避免仅按普通糖尿病解释；无麻木主诉与神经传导异常可同时存在；保留数值矛盾比悄悄修正更符合证据边界。尚待核对：缺少遗传检测、脂肪丢失前体重、血脂方法与复核、眼底原始结果及长期治疗结局；DXA百分比保留报告值。本稿尚未经执业医师审核。
