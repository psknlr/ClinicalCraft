## Learning outcomes

After the case the learner can:

1. Recognise haemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis in a child with prolonged fever, splenomegaly and cytopenias by
   the HLH-2004 criteria (Histiocyte Society, 2007) — five of eight here from the enlarged spleen, the blood count,
   triglycerides with fibrinogen, ferritin and the marrow — and explain why an EBV trigger does not exclude a primary
   (genetic) form.
2. Start HLH-94 induction (dexamethasone 10 mg/m²/day and etoposide 150 mg/m² twice weekly; Histiocyte Society
   consensus 2018) as soon as EBV-associated HLH is diagnosed, without waiting for genetic results and without
   substituting antivirals (Imashuku 2001; Chinese consensus on EBV-related disease in children 2021).
3. Interpret a gene report by zygosity and inheritance: one heterozygous UNC13D variant does not establish
   autosomal recessive FHL3, and a hemizygous SH2D1A variant must be written in HGVS, classified under ACMG/AMP
   2015 and supported by SAP and degranulation assays (NACHO 2019) before it means XLP1.
4. Refer for allogeneic haematopoietic stem cell transplantation when HLH reactivates after induction, whatever
   the genetic result (HLH-2004), and start an unrelated-donor search when there is no sibling donor.
5. Assess diarrhoea after transplantation for acute GVHD and infection in parallel, and start methylprednisolone
   2 mg/kg/day for grade II–IV gut GVHD while infection is investigated (EBMT 2024; HHV-6 per ECIL-7 2019).
6. Explain why post-transplant blood reflects the donor's genotype, use stored pre-transplant DNA or cultured skin
   fibroblasts for germline testing, and plan genetic counselling with testing of both parents.

## Clinical summary (spoilers)

**The report's diagnosis** (its title): familial haemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis with a MUNC13-4 (UNC13D)
mutation, treated with an unrelated-donor haematopoietic stem cell transplant.

**What the record shows.** A boy of 7 years 1 month had about a month of fever (called intermittent, recurrent and
persistent in different places) that treatment for an upper respiratory infection elsewhere did not settle; his
liver and spleen were found to be enlarged early in the illness. He has no siblings and no similar family history.
Examination: hepatomegaly and splenomegaly, without measurements. White cells 3.4 × 10⁹/L, neutrophils 0.84 (no
unit), platelets 50 × 10⁹/L, haemoglobin 71 g/L; ferritin 1176.6 µg/L, triglycerides 5.8 mmol/L, fibrinogen
1.3 g/L; marrow haemophagocytes; blood EBV DNA 5.43 × 10⁷ copies (no volume given); NK cells 1.9 %, with low
proportions of perforin- and granzyme B-positive cells. The gene report lists a heterozygous UNC13D (MUNC13-4)
c.1228A>C variant and a hemizygous SH2D1A variant whose notation is printed unclearly; no second UNC13D variant.
On an HLH protocol he went into remission, then showed signs of renewed activity, and had a transplant from a fully
HLA-matched unrelated donor. HHV-6-positive diarrhoea and clinical grade III intestinal GVHD followed and improved
with treatment. At 9 months: complete donor chimerism, disease-free survival, no chronic GVHD; blood genotyping
after the transplant reflected the donor's haematopoiesis.

**The teaching reading.**
* **HLH by criteria.** HLH-2004 needs five of eight criteria (or a molecular diagnosis). Splenomegaly; haemoglobin
  71 g/L with platelets 50 × 10⁹/L (two lineages); triglycerides 5.8 mmol/L with fibrinogen 1.3 g/L; ferritin
  1176.6 µg/L; marrow haemophagocytosis — five, without counting the fever (the sixth; no temperature is
  recorded). "NK cells 1.9 %" is a proportion, not the NK-activity criterion; soluble CD25 is not recorded.
* **EBV is the trigger, not the explanation.** EBV-HLH in a boy may be the first sign of an inherited disorder of
  lymphocyte cytotoxicity (FHL, XLP); the family history and the absence of siblings exclude nothing.
* **The genotype is not proven.** FHL3 is autosomal recessive: one heterozygous UNC13D variant does not establish
  it. A hemizygous SH2D1A variant in a boy would mean XLP1 if pathogenic, but its notation is unclear, its
  classification and any SAP/degranulation testing or maternal testing are not recorded. The report's title label
  (MUNC13-4-mutated familial HLH) would today need both UNC13D alleles — or the diagnosis is XLP1, or neither.
* **The flare decided the transplant.** HLH-2004 recommends transplantation for reactivated disease whatever the
  genotype; with no siblings, the donor search goes outside the family.
* **After the transplant**, a positive HHV-6 result and GVHD are worked up together; severe gut GVHD is treated with
  systemic steroids. A post-transplant blood genotype reads the donor, so germline testing needs pre-transplant DNA
  or a non-blood tissue.

### Common learner errors and how the case surfaces them

* **Calling it severe infectious mononucleosis** because the EBV load is huge — decision 1's
  `severe-mononucleosis`; the feedback counts the criteria.
* **Waiting for the genes before etoposide** — decision 2's `steroid-await-genes`; the critical item and the phase
  gate make the induction an act in `admission`.
* **Treating EBV with antivirals instead of HLH** — decision 2's `antiviral-ivig` and the `ganciclovir-ebv`
  intervention (contraindicated before etoposide).
* **Starting steroids or chemotherapy before the marrow** — contraindicated `dexamethasone`/`etoposide`; the
  critical item counts only doses after the marrow is read.
* **Reading one heterozygous variant as a diagnosis**, or an unclear SH2D1A variant as proof of XLP1 —
  decision 3's `fhl3-confirmed` and `xlp-no-transplant`.
* **Stopping at 8 weeks because "it was only EBV"** — decision 3's `secondary-complete-course`.
* **Treating the positive HHV-6 as the whole story**, or stopping immunosuppression to clear it — decision 4 and
  the contraindicated `withdraw-immunosuppression`.
* **Using post-transplant blood (or a cheek swab) for germline testing** — decision 5.

**学习目标：** 按HLH-2004标准识别HLH并认识EB病毒只是诱因；确诊后立即开始HLH-94诱导，不等基因、不以抗病毒药代替；按合子状态
和遗传方式解读UNC13D与SH2D1A变异（HGVS、ACMG/AMP 2015、SAP和脱颗粒功能检测）；病情再活动即转诊移植、启动非血缘供者检索；移植后
腹泻同时评估GVHD和感染，Ⅱ–Ⅳ度肠道GVHD用甲泼尼龙2 mg/kg/天；胚系检测用移植前DNA或培养的皮肤成纤维细胞并安排遗传咨询。

**关键决策：** ① 工作诊断：EB病毒诱发的HLH（满足≥5条），仍需查找遗传病因和淋巴瘤；② 初始治疗：现在就开始HLH-94并送基因和
脱颗粒检测；③ 再活动：再活动即移植指征，同时复核变异、做功能检测和父母检测；④ 移植后腹泻：GVHD与感染同时评估，Ⅱ度及以上即用全身
激素；⑤ 基因随访：用移植前或非血液来源的DNA，并检测父母。两个关键（critical）评分项都是行动：入院阶段在骨髓结果之后开始地塞米松和
依托泊苷；移植后阶段开始甲泼尼龙。
