## Decisions and assessment

- **diagnosis:** How should this renal-predominant TMA be differentiated? Options: Assess complement-associated HUS, infection-associated HUS and other TMAs (supported: Absent diarrhoea does not replace pathogen and complement evidence.) - **evidence:** Which conclusion is supported by the subsequent records? Options: Anti-factor-H positivity supports an immune complement mechanism, with limited titre/genetic data (supported: Normal ADAMTS13 argues against severe-deficiency TTP without excluding every other cause.) Low C3 alone diagnoses anti-factor-H disease (distractor: Antibody and clinical evidence are needed.) Routine negative stool culture fully excludes STEC (distractor: Specific toxin testing was not detailed.)

Normal counts permit permanent discharge from follow-up (distractor: Antibody and organ risks can persist.) Adjust immunosuppression independently from the old report (distractor: Infection, antibody and organ monitoring are required.)
