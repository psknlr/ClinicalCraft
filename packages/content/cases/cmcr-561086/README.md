## Clinical summary (spoilers)

Late-pregnancy haemoptysis progressed to postoperative haemothorax; surgery and pathology identified bleeding intralobar sequestration.

- Review obstetric and monitoring records: On day3 frequent haemoptysis and preterm labour led to emergency caesarean birth of a boy and girl. Afterwards tachycardia, falling oxygen saturation and falling Hb/Hct developed.
- Review chest CT and ultrasound: Left atelectasis and haemothorax; substantial bloody fluid remained after drainage of about1700 ml of dark nonclotting blood.
- Review thoracotomy findings: About800 ml clot, a ruptured approximately6 cm left lower-lobe cyst with active bleeding, and an approximately0.5 cm anomalous artery entering from below the diaphragm beneath the inferior pulmonary vein.
- Review lobectomy pathology: Diffuse alveolar haemorrhage, inflammation and fibrin with bronchial clot obstruction; final diagnosis was intralobar pulmonary sequestration with secondary pulmonary bleeding.

## 中文摘要

**病例概要：** 孕晚期咯血进展为术后大量血胸，手术和病理显示叶内型肺隔离症出血。

**学习目标：**

- 识别孕产期持续胸腔出血警讯。
- 解释异常供血支持的诊断。
- 组织多学科止血评估。
- 平衡妊娠影像收益和风险。

**关键决策：** 膈下进入病肺的体循环异常动脉支持肺隔离症；母体复苏与胸外科、产科、麻醉团队紧急协作，评估止血和血管解剖；按临床必要性权衡影像，不能仅因妊娠拒绝必要CT。

**复盘要点：** 妊娠高血压不能解释所有胸部急症。 异常体循环供血是本例关键解剖证据。 必要影像的延误也有风险。

原报告结局：结扎切断异常供血动脉并左下肺叶切除，术后第5天出院，报告母婴健康。异常动脉精确起源未由术前血管成像确认。
