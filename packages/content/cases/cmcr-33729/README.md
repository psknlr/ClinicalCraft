## Clinical summary (spoilers)

Granulicatella endocarditis with severe aortic valve injury; temporary infection improvement did not remove structural risk.

- Review pre-antibiotic blood cultures: Three cultures grew the same organism, identified by VITEK2 as Granulicatella adiacens, with satellitism.
- Review echocardiography: Aortic vegetations up to about 25 mm, prolapse and severe regurgitation, LVEF 48% and left-heart enlargement.
- Review susceptibilities: Vancomycin and some other agents were reported susceptible; erythromycin/clindamycin resistant; MICs are absent.
- Review preceding echocardiograms: Possible aortic vegetation and moderate regurgitation had already been reported before subsequent deterioration.

## Decisions and assessment

1. Does absent fever exclude infection here? Supported response: Cultures and vegetation support endocarditis despite initially absent fever Rationale: Direct microbiological and structural evidence is decisive.

2. What is needed for severe regurgitation and heart failure? Supported response: An endocarditis team urgently assesses surgery and targeted antibiotics Rationale: Heart failure and valve destruction should not simply await complete infection clearance.

3. How should later symptomatic improvement be interpreted? Supported response: It does not prove repaired valves or justify stopping therapy independently Rationale: Infection control, structural injury and treatment duration are distinct.

## 中文摘要

**病例概要：** 苛养颗粒链球菌心内膜炎合并严重主动脉瓣损伤，短期感染改善未解除结构风险。

**学习目标：**

- 整合培养与超声识别无热心内膜炎。
- 识别重度返流与心衰的手术紧迫性。
- 将过敏史纳入抗菌评估。
- 区别培养转阴、结构恢复与治愈。

**关键决策：** 培养与赘生物支持感染性心内膜炎，即使起初无发热；感染心内膜炎团队尽快评估外科及针对性抗菌治疗；不代表瓣膜已修复或疗程可自行中断。

**复盘要点：** 无热不排除心内膜炎。 心衰是及时外科评估的重要依据（ESC 2023）。 未证实的终末机制保留为可能。

原报告结局：使用万古霉素等后培养转阴并短期改善；转院后抗菌治疗中断，随后突发进行性气短并死亡。可能瓣膜破坏、栓塞或急性左心衰，具体机制未证实。
