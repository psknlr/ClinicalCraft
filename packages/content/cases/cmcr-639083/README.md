## Decisions and assessment

- **diagnosis:** What mechanism is supported by the critical sample? Options: Unsuppressed insulin with low ketones during hypoglycaemia supports hyperinsulinism (supported: Interpret hormones during hypoglycaemia, not by ordinary fasting ranges.) A modest insulin number excludes hyperinsulinism (distractor: Insulin should be suppressed during low glucose; absolute magnitude is not sufficient.) Term birth proves hypoglycaemia is transient (distractor: Recurrent persistence and response require reassessment.)
- **evidence:** Which conclusion is supported by the subsequent records? Options: Functional localisation suggests a focus; genetics require tissue correlation rather than predicting all anatomy (supported: Normal ultrasound does not negate functional localisation.) Normal ultrasound excludes a focal pancreatic lesion (distractor: Small functional lesions may be occult.) 18F-DOPA is universally 100% accurate (distractor: The report’s general statement does not establish universal accuracy.)
- **management:** How should localisation and treatment proceed safely? Options: Prevent hypoglycaemic brain injury while specialists plan medication, fasting support for investigations and focal surgery (supported: Do not undertake unsupervised fasting or copy historical doses.) Use prolonged fasting at home to confirm hypoglycaemia (distractor: This can cause severe neurological injury.) Any gene variant requires total pancreatectomy (distractor: Focal and diffuse disease require different strategies.)
