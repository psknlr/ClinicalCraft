## Clinical summary (spoilers)

Coronary occlusion in dextrocardia cannot be localised solely from standard ECG assumptions, and short-term ST resolution does not establish recovery.

- Review emergency ECG: Paroxysmal AF, VT and ST elevation in anterior leads.
- Review angiography: Left main stenosis 70%, diffuse LAD disease up to 85% with TIMI 3 flow; complete proximal RCA occlusion with local thrombus.
- Review chest film and structural evidence: Chest film showed mirror-image dextrocardia and cardiomegaly; echo and CT were not completed, leaving detailed anatomy uncertain.

## 中文摘要

**病例概要：** 右位心背景下的冠脉闭塞不能仅凭常规心电图定位，短期 ST 回落不代表恢复。

**学习目标：**

- 识别右侧心尖提示的解剖异常。
- 以造影修正罪犯血管判断。
- 兼顾急诊处理与解剖核对。
- 避免把 ST 改善等同于存活。

**关键决策：** 结合造影，RCA 闭塞比仅凭前壁导联推定 LAD 更有依据；紧急心脏团队处理缺血，同时核对右位心导联与解剖；不能，只有短期 ST 回落且随后严重恶化，离院后结局未知。

**复盘要点：** 右位心需要核对导联与解剖。 冠脉血流证据修正最初定位。 分别记录电图、器官功能和最终结局。
