## Clinical summary (spoilers)

Final pathology diagnosed peritoneal mesothelioma with extreme mature neutrophilia, requiring distinction between infection and tumour-related reaction.

- Review abdominal imaging: A left lower mixed cystic/solid mass around 7.1×6.3×9.9 cm, blurred surrounding fat and abdominopelvic fluid; Doppler showed vascularity.
- Review bowel relationship studies: Colonoscopy was unremarkable; small-bowel contrast showed extrinsic compression without leakage, supporting an extraintestinal mass.
- Review counts and marrow: WBC rose from 28.8 to 33.3×10⁹/L, predominantly mature neutrophils; marrow showed active granulopoiesis without obvious atypia. Two blood cultures were negative.
- Review exploration and final pathology: A mesenteric-root mass with widespread nodules; frozen section suggested metastatic adenocarcinoma, but final tissue/immunophenotype diagnosed malignant mesothelioma: calretinin/CK18/vimentin positive and CEA negative.
