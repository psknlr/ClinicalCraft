## Clinical summary

The source diagnosed chronic active EBV infection with CNS T-lymphoproliferative disease, while modern infected-lineage confirmation remains missing. Long course and systemic disease argue against one isolated encephalitis. PCR and cell phenotype need linkage at the infected-cell level. Absent haemophagocytosis does not exclude the syndrome.

## Decisions and assessment

- **diagnosis:** How should years of systemic and CNS findings be framed? Options: Assess persistent EBV-associated systemic disease and a lymphoproliferative process rather than a single self-limited encephalitis (supported: Longstanding fever, organomegaly and viral evidence warrant broader assessment.) Positive oligoclonal bands alone establish isolated multiple sclerosis (distractor: This does not explain the systemic and viral findings.) Five years of illness can be treated as one uncomplicated acute infection (distractor: Recurrence and progression require reassessment.)
- **evidence:** Which conclusion is supported by the subsequent records? Options: The report supports CAEBV and a T-cell-associated process, but tissue PCR plus T-cell infiltration does not directly establish the infected lineage (supported: Modern diagnosis requires more specific evidence of infected cells; missing data cannot be invented.) Absent haemophagocytes permanently exclude HLH (distractor: Negative morphology alone does not replace the full syndrome assessment.) EBER/T-cell double staining is proven positive (distractor: The source did not provide that test.)
- **management:** How should a current specialist pathway be planned? Options: Haematology, infection and neurology teams should refine viral quantification and infected-cell typing and assess definitive therapy plus CNS support (supported: The 2023 guideline emphasises complete diagnosis and specialist treatment; historical antiviral or B-cell-targeted courses cannot be generalised.) Repeat historical rituximab for every T-cell-associated disease (distractor: Drug target and infected-cell lineage require reconciliation.) Temporary defervescence proves cure by antiviral treatment alone (distractor: Temporary symptom control does not establish eradication of the abnormal cellular process.)
