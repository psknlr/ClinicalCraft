## Decisions and assessment

- **diagnosis:** How should extreme leucocytosis with abundant blasts be interpreted? Options: Strongly suspect malignant blast proliferation requiring urgent haematological classification (supported: Persistently extreme counts, blast predominance and organ enlargement support this approach.) Attribute everything to birth stress without further assessment (distractor: Birth stress does not adequately explain counts around 500 × 10^9/L with over 90% blasts.) Diagnose bacterial infection from leucocytosis alone (distractor: A count cannot replace cell morphology and population assessment.)
- **evidence:** Which conclusion is supported by the subsequent records? Options: B-lineage markers support B-lymphoblastic leukaemia; negative screening does not exclude every genetic abnormality (supported: CD19 and cytoplasmic CD79a support lineage; negative screening covers only tested targets.) Negative POX proves acute myeloid leukaemia (distractor: The immunophenotype indicates B lineage; POX cannot be interpreted in reverse.) A normal karyotype establishes the absence of genetic factors (distractor: Conventional karyotyping and limited fusion panels have detection limits.)
- **management:** How should the next safe course be discussed with the family? Options: Urgent neonatal-haematology assessment with clear discussion of risks, options and uncertainties (supported: Infancy and extreme leucocytosis are high-risk features requiring specialist assessment.) Use routine follow-up because responsiveness is initially good (distractor: A relatively stable appearance does not negate the haematological danger signals.) Declare all modern treatment futile from this old case outcome (distractor: The report did not document full treatment; its outcome cannot represent current infant ALL care.)

## 中文摘要

**病例概要：** 原报告诊断先天性B淋巴细胞白血病，极高白细胞、肝脾大和B系免疫表型构成关键证据。

**学习目标：** 识别极高白细胞与原始细胞优势的紧急性；由B系免疫表型解释白血病分系；说明阴性融合筛查的检测边界；以家属可理解的方式说明专科评估和不确定性

**关键决策：** 极高白细胞伴大量原始细胞应优先怎样解释？ 后续资料能支持哪一项结论？ 如何向家属讨论接下来的安全方向？

**复盘要点：** 原始细胞优势比单独白细胞计数更有分类意义；阴性分子筛查不能超出其靶标范围解释；早期外观好不能排除严重血液病。尚待核对：结节病理、完整分子分型、现代方案治疗反应及死因。本稿尚未经执业医师审核。
