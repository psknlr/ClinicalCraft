## Clinical summary (spoilers)

Divided overdose of a combination analgesic preceded severe hepatocellular injury; key lessons are ingredient reconciliation, urgent toxicology assessment and dosing communication.

- Review formulation and dispensing: Each tablet contained paracetamol 500 mg and dihydrocodeine 10 mg; twenty tablets amount to 10 g paracetamol in divided ingestions.
- Review liver-test timeline: Preoperative ALT 12 and AST 18 U/L; ALT later rose through 112 to 1128 U/L and continued rising.
- Review hepatitis testing: Tests for hepatitis A, B, C and E supplied no positive evidence supporting acute infection.
- Review severity information: Paracetamol concentration, INR, glucose and encephalopathy assessment are absent; aminotransferases alone do not establish acute liver failure.

## 中文摘要

**病例概要：** 复方镇痛药分次超量后出现严重肝细胞损伤，核心教学是成分核对、及时中毒评估和用药沟通。

**学习目标：**

- 按成分计算累计对乙酰氨基酚量。
- 识别肝损伤需立即中毒专科评估。
- 区别肝细胞损伤与急性肝衰竭。
- 用复述确认药物间隔和累计量沟通。

**关键决策：** 复方镇痛药中对乙酰氨基酚累计超量；立即停可疑复方药并联系中毒/肝病团队，评估 NAC、凝血、肾功能和脑病；支持暴露相关肝损伤，但不能证明每种“保肝”药有效或没有 NAC 需求。

**复盘要点：** 应累计所有复方制剂中的同一成分。 2023 中毒共识支持依据时序、浓度及肝损伤评估 NAC。 分次摄入及合并阿片会影响浓度解释，不能机械套图。

原报告结局：ALT 峰值 2510 U/L；原报告多种支持治疗后下降，约 3 周后 ALT 34、AST 18 U/L。未记载 NAC 使用，不能将缺失写成已给药。
